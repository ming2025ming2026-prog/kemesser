TransHome Dry Ice Transport Container

For low-temperature sample transport and transfer using dry ice. A separate sample compartment, power-free design and refillable dry ice help extend temperature retention.

Request Product Information

PRODUCT FEATURES

Product Features

01

Dry-ice Isolation

Samples do not contact dry ice directly, making loading and retrieval easier with less disruption to handling.

02

Portable Power-free Design

No external power is required, making the container suitable for transfers within the laboratory and transport outside it.

03

Extended Temperature Retention

Extended low-temperature retention helps protect samples during transport.

04

Efficient Space Use

The compact design provides more usable space for managing batches of samples.

05

Refillable Dry Ice

Dry ice can be added during transport to extend temperature retention.

06

Flexible Multi-purpose Use

Can be used for transport or as a mobile, dry-ice-cooled ultra-low-temperature storage container.
