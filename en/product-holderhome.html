LOW-TEMPERATURE HANDLING SERIES

HolderHome Ice-free Low-temperature Container

For short-term low-temperature sample storage and transfer during experiments. Pre-cooled cooling cores maintain a stable temperature range, reducing contamination, moisture exposure and temperature fluctuations associated with conventional ice baths.

Request Product Information

PRODUCT FEATURES

Product Features

01

No Direct Wet-ice Contact

Separating samples from wet ice reduces water exposure, damp labels and the risk of tube contamination.

02

Consistent Cooling Conditions

Each tube is held under consistent low-temperature conditions for more consistent sample processing results.

03

Upright Organized Samples

Tubes remain upright with labels visible for easy identification, loading and retrieval.

04

Transfer Protection

Samples remain protected during handling and transfer, reducing contamination and damage risks.

05

Reusable Design

Both the container and cooling cores can be reused, reducing disposable consumables and the burden of wet-ice management.

06

Flexible Module Compatibility

The container accommodates tube modules or freezing racks to suit different samples and workflows.

SPECIFICATIONS

Temperature Ranges and Specifications

HolderHome-X1-20–0°C

Temperature retention: up to 12 hours

HolderHome-X2-22–-18°C

Temperature retention: up to 5 hours

HolderHome-XD-78°C
