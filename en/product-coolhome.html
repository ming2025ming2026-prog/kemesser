LOW-TEMPERATURE HANDLING SERIES

CoolHome Ice-free Workstation

Designed for sample cooling and standardized low-temperature handling without wet ice to help reduce contamination risks. Cooling cores with different temperature ranges meet a variety of experimental needs.

Request Product Information

PRODUCT FEATURES

Product Features

01

No Direct Wet-ice Contact

Keeping samples separate from wet ice reduces water exposure, damp labels and contamination risks associated with tubes shifting or sinking.

02

Consistent Cooling Rate

Each tube receives consistent cooling conditions for more uniform temperature control and standardized procedures.

03

Upright Organized Samples

Tubes remain upright with visible labels for easier identification, handling and workflow organization.

04

Reusable Design

The workstation can be reused without a continuous supply of wet ice, reducing routine maintenance such as cleaning up water.

05

Extended Temperature Control

Cooling cores maintain low temperatures for sample processing, short-term holding and bench-to-bench transfer.

06

Modular Compatibility

Compatible BlockHome modules support multiple tube types, plates and sample containers.
