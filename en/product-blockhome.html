BlockHome Tube Module

A modular tube holder designed to organize, stabilize and thermally manage different sample formats during low-temperature operations.

Request Product Information

PRODUCT FEATURES

Product Features

01

Temperature Uniformity

The module keeps samples at a uniform temperature below 4°C, with temperature uniformity of ±0.1°C.

02

Upright Samples

Different sample formats can be held upright without shifting or sinking into ice.

03

Clear Indexing

Structured positions make samples easier to identify, track and retrieve.

04

Dry and Safe

Dry handling reduces water contamination and label damage associated with wet ice.

05

Temperature Reading

Compare the module's color with a standard color chart to read the temperature, with a reading error within ±0.5°C.

06

Reusable Multi-format Design

Reusable modules offer multiple well configurations and are compatible with CoolHome and HolderHome.
