MobiThaw Portable Cell Thawing System

A compact, rechargeable water-free thawing system with data export, temperature protection and real-time alerts for mobile or flexible laboratory use.

Request Product Information

FUNCTIONS

Feature Configuration

01

Ozone Disinfection

One-touch ozone disinfection helps maintain a clean operating environment and reduce contamination risk.

02

Adjustable Residual Ice

Users can adjust the residual ice at the end of thawing to suit their experimental requirements.

03

USB Type-C Charging

USB Type-C charging supports flexible use across mobile and temporary laboratory settings.

04

SD Card Data Storage

Insert the SD card only when the instrument is powered off; hot swapping is not supported. Data recording starts automatically at power-on, with records retained and backed up for later review.

05

Compact and Portable

A compact body is easy to carry and operate in space-limited or mobile scenarios.

06

Water-free Thawing

Automated dry thawing avoids direct water contact, reducing contamination risk and simplifying operation.

07

Color Touchscreen

A clear color touchscreen provides intuitive control and real-time process status.

08

Temperature Protection

The protection temperature can be set from -40 to 0°C to maintain the sample's protection temperature while the device is idle.

09

Data Export

Recorded process data can be exported for archiving, analysis and traceability.

10

Temperature Calibration

Temperature calibration supports stable temperature performance and repeatable experimental results.

11

Real-time Alerts

Process monitoring and alerts help operators respond promptly to abnormal conditions.
