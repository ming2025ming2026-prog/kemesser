Consistent Cryopreservation Workflows

No Auxiliary Materials Required

No auxiliary materials are required, helping save energy and support environmentally friendly, efficient operation.

02

Lower Long-term Cost

A single purchase provides long-term use, helping reduce costs.

03

High Repeatability

Cooling rates are consistent from run to run and across all well positions, providing uniform, highly repeatable freezing.

04

Easy Handling

Easy to use and operate by hand, without tools.

05

Non-contaminating, Insulated Design

The solid, thermally insulated design requires no pre-cooling. When handling the container in or out of a -80°C freezer, use suitable cold-protection gloves in accordance with laboratory safety procedures and avoid direct skin contact with cold surfaces.

06

Easy Lid Opening

The purpose-designed lid is easy to open whenever needed.

07

Rack-level Transfer

Samples can be transferred together rather than removed one by one.
