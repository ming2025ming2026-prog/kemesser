Water-free Thawing

Place the sample in the corresponding thawing slot for automated water-free thawing, reducing water-related contamination risk and manual handling errors.

Telescopic Heated Lid

The telescopic heated lid accommodates samples of different capacities and shapes. Four-direction air convection improves temperature uniformity during thawing.

Real-time process monitoring and alerts help reduce operating errors that could affect cell viability.
